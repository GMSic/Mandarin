This app searches two bundled open datasets: CC-CEDICT for words, English definitions and pinyin, and Make Me a Hanzi for character definitions, radicals and decomposition.

Type English, Chinese, or pinyin to see suggestions. Select a word, then a character. Expand “Radicals & components” to see the available source explanation.

Sentences are matched to dictionary phrases and words using longest-match segmentation. These are literal study glosses: grammar, context, word order and pronunciation can be ambiguous. Unknown words remain visible. English results are dictionary choices, not composed Chinese sentences.

Components can suggest meaning or sound. They are not all radicals, and modern shapes do not always explain historical origins. Missing information is marked rather than invented.

Data licenses: CC-CEDICT · CC BY-SA 4.0; Make Me a Hanzi · LGPL 3+. No text is sent to a translation service. Optional audio uses your device’s speech service, which may use a network voice.
